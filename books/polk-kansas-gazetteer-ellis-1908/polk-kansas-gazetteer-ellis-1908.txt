From R. L. Polk & Co.'s Kansas State Gazetteer and Business Directory, Volume XI (Detroit, 1908).

> The Ellis County place entries of the Gazetteer Department of Polk's Kansas State Gazetteer and Business Directory, Volume XI (1908–09) — every Ellis County post office, town, and settlement the volume lists, gathered here as one county record, reprinted as printed, with display advertising and page furniture removed. The volume's three scanned parts (archive.org: `rlpolkcoskansass21rlpo`, `rlpolkcoskansass22rlpo`, `rlpolkcoskansass23rlpo`) have been checked entry by entry; the places named here are the whole of the county's representation in that volume.


* * *



A Note on This Extraction

The Gazetteer Department of the volume lists some 1,486 Kansas towns, villages, post offices, and settlements alphabetically. The fourteen Ellis County entries in this volume follow below. Two cautions. First, the county as Polk draws it includes places that have since vanished (Bantam, Mendota, Norfolk, Finch, Turkville) — they stand here as they stood in 1908. Second, Liebenthal does not appear: the volume lists it as a Rush County post office, and it is therefore not an Ellis County entry. Toulon and Yocemento (real Ellis County settlements of the era, named in Blackmar's 1912 Cyclopedia) have no entries in this volume; they are recorded here as absent, not silently passed over.
