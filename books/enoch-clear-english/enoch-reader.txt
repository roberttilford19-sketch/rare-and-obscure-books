To the memory of Robert Henry Charles (1855–1931), scholar of the Ethiopic text.

This edition stands on his work: his edition of the Ge'ez manuscripts, and his English translation of 1912. Without them, the First Book of Enoch would have had no road into this editor's hands, and none into plain English by this road. The rendering is ours. The bedrock is his.

Chapter 1

1. These are the words of Enoch's blessing. With them he blessed God's chosen, righteous people, who will live in the day of trouble, when all the wicked and godless will be taken away.
2. He began his parable and said: I am Enoch, a righteous man. God opened my eyes, and I saw a vision of the Holy One in heaven. The angels showed it to me. From them I heard everything, and I understood what I saw. It is not for this generation, but for a far-off generation still to come.
3. I spoke about the chosen ones, and I began my parable about them: The Holy Great One* will come out from where He lives,
4. and the eternal God will step onto the earth, on Mount Sinai, and He will appear in His full strength from the highest heaven.
5. Everyone will be struck with fear. The Watchers will shake with fear. Great fear and trembling will take hold of them to the ends of the earth.
6. The high mountains will shake. The high hills will be brought low, and will melt like wax in a flame.
7. The earth will be torn completely apart. Everything on the earth will die. There will be judgment on everyone.
8. But with the righteous He will make peace. He will protect His chosen ones, and He will show them mercy. They will all belong to God. They will do well. They will all be blessed. He will help them all. Light will appear for them, and He will make peace with them.
9. Look! He comes with tens of thousands of His holy ones, to judge everyone, to destroy all the ungodly, and to convict all people of every ungodly deed they have done, and of every hard word* that ungodly sinners have spoken against Him.

Chapter 2

1. Watch everything that happens in the sky. See how the lights in the sky do not change their paths. They all rise and set in order, each in its season, and they do not break the order set for them.
2. Look at the earth. Pay attention to the things that happen on it, from first to last. See how steady they hold, how nothing on earth changes, but all of God's works show themselves to you.
3. Look at the summer and the winter. See how the whole earth is filled with water, and how clouds, dew, and rain rest on it.

Chapter 3

1. Watch and see how, in winter, all the trees look as though they have withered and dropped all their leaves — except fourteen trees. Those trees do not lose their leaves. They keep their old leaves for two to three years, until the new leaves come.

Chapter 4

1. Again, watch the days of summer. See how the sun stands above the earth, facing it. You look for shade and shelter because of the sun's heat. The ground, too, burns with glowing heat, and you cannot walk on the ground or on a rock because of its heat.

Chapter 5

1. Watch how the trees cover themselves with green leaves and bear fruit. So pay attention and know, about all His works, and see that He who lives forever made them so.
2. All His works go on this way from year to year, forever. They carry out for Him all their tasks, and their tasks do not change — but as God has ordained, so it is done.
3. And see how the sea and the rivers do the same: they carry out their tasks and do not change, by His commandments.
4. But you — you have not been steadfast, and you have not done the Lord's commandments. You turned away and spoke proud, hard words with your unclean mouths against His greatness. O you hard-hearted ones, you shall find no peace.
5. So you will curse your days, and the years of your life will perish, and the years of your destruction will be multiplied in endless cursing, and you shall find no mercy.
6a. In those days you will make your names an eternal curse to all the righteous, b. and by you all who curse will curse, and all the sinners and the godless will curse by your name; 7c. and for you, the godless, there shall be a curse. 6d. And all the ... shall rejoice, e. and there shall be forgiveness of sins, f. and every mercy and peace and forbearance: g. there shall be salvation for them, a good light. i. And for all of you sinners there shall be no salvation, j. but on all of you shall rest a curse.
7a. But for the elect there shall be light and grace and peace, b. and they shall inherit the earth.
8. And then wisdom shall be given to the elect, and they shall all live and never sin again — not through godlessness and not through pride. But those who are wise shall be humble.
9. They shall not transgress again, nor sin all the days of their life, nor die by the divine anger or wrath. They shall live out the full number of the days of their life, and their lives shall grow in peace, and the years of their joy shall be multiplied, in endless gladness and peace, all the days of their life.

Chapter 6

1. It happened that when the children of men had grown many, in those days beautiful daughters were born to them.
2. And the angels, the children of heaven, saw them and lusted after them, and said to one another: 'Come, let us choose wives for ourselves from the children of men, and have children by them.'
3. And Semjaza, who was their leader, said to them: 'I fear you will not really agree to do this deed, and I alone will have to pay the penalty for a great sin.'
4. And they all answered him and said: 'Let us all swear an oath, and bind ourselves with mutual curses not to abandon this plan, but to do this thing.'
5. Then they all swore together, and bound themselves with mutual curses to it.
6. And they were two hundred in all. They came down, in the days of Jared, on the top of Mount Hermon, and they called it Mount Hermon, because they had sworn and bound themselves with mutual curses on it.
7. And these are the names of their leaders: Semiazaz, their leader, Araqiba, Rameel, Kokabel, Tamiel, Ramiel, Danel, Ezeqeel, Baraqijal, Asael, Armaros, Batarel, Ananel, Zaqiel, Samsapeel, Satarel, Turel, Jomjael, Sariel.
8. These are their chiefs of tens.

Chapter 7

1. And all the rest of them, together with the leaders, took wives for themselves, and each chose one for himself. And they began to go in to them and to make themselves unclean with them. And they taught them charms and enchantments, the cutting of roots, and showed them the plants.
2. And they became pregnant, and gave birth to great giants, whose height was three thousand ells:
3. They ate up all that men possessed. And when men could no longer feed them,
4. the giants turned on them and devoured mankind.
5. And they began to wrong the birds, the beasts, the reptiles, and the fish, and to eat one another's flesh and drink one another's blood.
6. Then the earth cried out, accusing the lawless ones.

Chapter 8

1. And Azâzêl taught men to make swords, and knives, and shields, and breastplates. And he showed them the metals of the earth, and the craft of working them: bracelets, and ornaments, and the use of antimony, and the painting of the eyes, and all kinds of costly stones, and all colouring dyes.
2. And much godlessness rose up, and they committed fornication, and they were led astray, and grew corrupt in all their ways.
3. Semjâzâ taught spells and root-cuttings; Armârôs taught the undoing of spells; Barâqîjâl taught astrology; Kôkabêl taught the constellations; Ezêqêêl taught the knowledge of the clouds; ⟨Araqiêl the signs of the earth, Shamsiêl the signs of the sun⟩; and Sariêl taught the course of the moon.
4. And as men were dying out, they cried aloud, and their cry went up to heaven. . . .

Chapter 9

1. Then Michael, Uriel, Raphael, and Gabriel looked down from heaven. They saw much blood being shed on the earth, and all lawlessness being worked on the earth.
2. And they said to one another: "The earth made †without inhabitant cries the voice of their crying† up to the gates of heaven.
(Note: the mark † is Charles's. It stands where the ancient text has come down to us damaged or uncertain, so the reader can see where the ground is less firm. It is kept wherever Charles put it, here and through the whole book.)
3. ⌜⌜And now to you, the holy ones of heaven⌝⌝, the souls of men make their plea, saying, 'Bring our cause before the Most High.'"
4. And they said to the Lord of the ages: "Lord of lords, God of gods, King of kings, ⟨and God of the ages⟩ — the throne of Your glory stands through all the generations of the ages, and Your name is holy and glorious and blessed through all the ages!
5. You have made all things, and power over all things is Yours. All things lie open and naked before Your eyes. You see everything, and nothing can hide from You.
6. You see what Azâzêl has done — he has taught all unrighteousness on earth, and revealed the eternal secrets that were kept in heaven, which men were striving to learn.
7. And Semjâzâ, to whom You have given authority to rule over his companions.
8. And they went to the daughters of men on the earth, and slept with the women, and defiled themselves, and showed them every kind of sin.
9. And the women bore giants, and because of them the whole earth was filled with blood and unrighteousness.
10. And now, look — the souls of those who have died are crying out and bringing their plea to the gates of heaven, and their cries of grief have gone up, and cannot cease.
11. And You know all things before they happen. You see these things, and You let them go on, and You do not tell us what we are to do with them."

Chapter 10

1. Then the Most High spoke — the Holy and Great One — and sent **Uriel** to the son of Lamech, and said to him:
2. "⟨Go to Noah and⟩ tell him in my name, 'Hide yourself!' Show him the end that is coming: the whole earth will be destroyed, and a **flood** is about to come on the whole earth and destroy everything on it.
3. Teach him now, so he can escape and his line can be kept alive through all the generations of the world."
4. And again the Lord said to Raphael: "Tie up Azâzêl hand and foot and throw him into the darkness. Open up a pit in the desert — in Dûdâêl — and throw him into it.
5. Pile rough, jagged rocks on him, cover him with darkness, and let him stay there for ever. Cover his face so he cannot see light.
6. On the day of the great judgement he will be thrown into the fire.
7. Heal the earth that the angels have ruined, and proclaim the healing of the earth, so they can heal the plague and none of the children of men perish because of all the secret things the Watchers have **disclosed** and taught their sons.
8. The whole earth has been ruined by the works Azâzêl taught. Lay all sin on him."
9. And to Gabriel the Lord said: "Move against the bastards and the outcasts, and against the children of fornication. Destroy [the children of fornication and] the children of the Watchers from among men [and cause them to go forth]: send them against each other so they kill each other in battle, for they will not live long.
10. No plea they make to you — their fathers — will be granted to their fathers for them, for they hope to live for ever, and each of them to live five hundred years."
11. And the Lord said to Michael: "Go, tie up Semjâzâ and his companions, who joined themselves to women and defiled themselves with them in all their uncleanness.
12. When their sons have killed each other and they have seen their loved ones destroyed, tie them fast for seventy generations in the **valleys** of the earth, till the day of their judgement and their end, till the judgement that lasts for ever and ever is complete.
13. In those days they will be led away to the lake of fire — ⟨and⟩ to the torment and the prison where they will be shut up for ever.
14. And whoever is **condemned** and destroyed will from then on be tied up with them to the end of all generations.
15. Destroy all the spirits of the outcasts and the children of the Watchers, because they have wronged mankind.
16. Wipe all wrong off the face of the earth and bring every evil work to an end. Let the plant of righteousness and truth appear: ⌜it will be a blessing — the works of righteousness and truth⌝ will be planted in truth and joy for evermore.
17. Then all who do right will escape,
and live till they have thousands of children,
and complete all the days of their youth and their old age in peace.
18. Then the whole earth will be farmed in righteousness, planted with trees everywhere, and full of blessing.
19. Every good tree will be planted on it, and they will plant vines on it. The vine they plant there will yield wine in plenty, and for every seed sown there each measure will bear a thousand, and each measure of olives will yield ten presses of oil.
20. Cleanse the earth of all oppression, all unrighteousness, all sin, all godlessness. Destroy from off the earth all the uncleanness worked on the earth.
21. ⌜And all the children of men will become righteous⌝ — all nations will bow down and praise Me, and all will worship Me.
22. The earth will be cleansed of all defilement, all sin, all punishment, all torment, and I will never again send (them) on it, from generation to generation, for ever."

Chapter 11

1. In those days I will open the storehouses of blessing in heaven, and send them down ⌜on the earth⌝, over the work and labour of the children of men.
2. Truth and peace will go together through all the days of the world and through all the generations of men."

Chapter 12

1. Before all this, Enoch was hidden. None of the children of men knew where he was hidden, where he stayed, or what had become of him.
2. His work had to do with the Watchers, and his days were spent with the holy ones.
3. And I, Enoch, was blessing the Lord of majesty and the King of the ages — and look! The Watchers called me, Enoch the scribe, and said to me:
4. "Enoch, you scribe of righteousness, go — †tell† the Watchers of heaven, who left the high heaven, the holy eternal place, and defiled themselves with women, doing as the children of earth do, taking wives for themselves: 'You have wrecked great ruin on the earth.
5. You will have no peace and no forgiveness of sin. And because †you† take delight in †your† children,
6. †you† will watch †your† loved ones murdered, and †you† will weep over †your† children destroyed, and plead for ever — but mercy and peace you will never get.'"

Chapter 13

1. And Enoch went and said: "Azâzêl, you will have no peace. A harsh sentence has gone out against you, to put you in chains.
2. You will get no mercy and no †hearing† for what you taught — all the wrong, the godlessness, the unrighteousness, the sin you showed to men."
3. Then I went and spoke to them all together. They were terrified — fear and trembling gripped them.
4. They begged me to write a plea for them, so they might find forgiveness, and to read their plea before the Lord of heaven.
5. For from then on they could not speak (with Him) or lift their eyes to heaven, ashamed of the sins they were condemned for.
6. So I wrote out their plea — the prayer †for their spirits ⌜and what each of them did⌝, asking for forgiveness and long life†.
7. Then I went off and sat by the waters of Dan, in the land of Dan, south-west of Hermon. I read their plea till I fell asleep.
8. And a dream came to me — visions fell on me. I saw visions of punishment, ⌜and a voice told (me)⌉ to take it to the sons of heaven and rebuke them.
9. When I woke, I went to them. They were all sitting together, weeping at 'Abelsjâil, between Lebanon and Sênêsêr, their faces covered.
10. I told them all the visions I had seen in my sleep, and began to speak words of righteousness and rebuke the heavenly Watchers.

Chapter 14

1. The book of the words of righteousness, and of the rebuke to the eternal Watchers, commanded by the Holy Great One in that vision.
2. In my sleep I saw what I will now say with a tongue of flesh, with the breath of my mouth — which the Great One gave to men, to talk with and understand with the heart.
3. As He made man and gave ⌜him the power to understand the word of wisdom, so He made me too and gave⌝ me the power to rebuke the Watchers, the children of heaven.
4. I wrote out your plea. In my vision it appeared like this: your plea will not be granted ⌜for all eternity — judgement has been passed on you for good, and (your plea) will not be granted⌝.
5. From now on you will never go up into heaven again, and ⌜in chains⌝ on earth the order has gone out to tie you up for all the days of the world.
6. Before that, you will have watched your loved sons destroyed — you will take no joy in them; they will fall before you by the sword.
7. Your plea for them will not be granted, nor your own — not though you weep and pray and speak every word written in the document I have written.
8. The vision came to me like this: Look — in the vision, clouds called me, mist summoned me. The stars and lightnings sped me on their course and hurried me along; the winds in the vision made me fly, lifted me up, and carried me into heaven.
9. I went in till I came near a wall built of crystal, ringed with tongues of fire — and it frightened me.
10. I went into the tongues of fire and came near a great house built of crystal. Its walls were like a crystal mosaic floor, and its foundation was crystal.
11. Its ceiling was like the path of the stars and the lightnings; fiery cherubim stood between them, and their heaven was (clear as) water.
12. Flaming fire ringed the walls, and its gates blazed with fire.
13. I entered that house — hot as fire and cold as ice. No joy of life was in it. Fear covered me; trembling gripped me.
14. Shaking and trembling, I fell on my face. And I saw a vision —
15. and look! There was a second house, greater than the first. Its whole doorway stood open before me, built of flames of fire.
16. In every way it outshone the first in splendour and grandeur and size — I cannot describe its splendour and its size to you.
17. Its floor was fire; above it were lightnings and the path of the stars; its ceiling too was flaming fire.
18. I looked and saw ⌜there⌝ a high throne. It looked like crystal; its wheels like the shining sun; and there was the vision of cherubim.
19. From under the throne streamed rivers of flaming fire, so I could not look at it.
20. The Great Glory sat on it. His clothing shone brighter than the sun, whiter than any snow.
21. No angel could enter or see His face, for His magnificence and glory — no flesh could look on Him.
22. Flaming fire circled Him; a great fire stood before Him. None around could come near Him. Ten thousand times ten thousand (stood) before Him — yet He needed no counsellor.
23. The most holy ones near Him never left by night, never went from Him.
24. Till then I had lain face down, trembling. And the Lord called me with His own mouth: "Come here, Enoch, and hear my word."
25. ⌜And one of the holy ones came to me and woke me⌝ — He made me stand and come to the door, and I bowed my face down.

Chapter 15

1. He answered me — I heard His voice: "Do not fear, Enoch, you righteous man, you scribe of righteousness. Come here and hear my voice.
2. Go, say to ⌜the Watchers of heaven⌝, who sent you to plead ⌜for them — 'You should plead⌝ for men, not men for you:
3. Why did you leave the high, holy, eternal heaven? You lay with women, defiled yourselves with the daughters of men, took wives, acted like the children of earth, and fathered giants (as your) sons.
4. Though you were holy — spiritual, living the eternal life — you defiled yourselves with the blood of women, fathered (children) with the blood of flesh, and, like the children of men, lusted after flesh and blood, as those ⌜also⌝ do who die and perish.
5. That is why I gave them wives — to impregnate them and have children by them, so nothing would be lacking to them on earth.
6. But you were ⌜once⌝ spiritual, living the eternal life, immortal for all generations of the world.
7. So I have not given wives to you — for the spiritual ones of heaven, heaven is their home.
8. And now the giants, born of spirit and flesh, will be called evil spirits on the earth — on the earth will be their home.
9. Evil spirits came out of their bodies. They are born of men, ⌜and⌝ from the holy Watchers is their beginning and first origin — ⌜they will be evil spirits on earth, and⌝ evil spirits they will be called.
[10. As for the spirits of heaven, heaven will be their home; but as for the spirits of the earth, born on the earth, the earth will be their home.]
11. The spirits of the giants afflict, oppress, destroy, attack, fight, and wreck ruin on the earth, and stir up trouble. They eat no food, ⌜yet hunger⌝ and thirst, and cause offence.
12. These spirits will rise against the children of men and against the women, because they came ⌜from them⌝."

Chapter 16

1. From the days of slaughter and ruin and death ⌜of the giants⌝ — the spirits that left the souls of their flesh will destroy and face no judgement. They will keep destroying till the day of the end, the great ⌜judgement⌝ when the age ends, over the Watchers and the godless — yes, wholly ended."
2. And now, for the Watchers who sent you to plead for them — who were ⌜once⌝ in heaven — (say to them):
3. "You were in heaven, but ⌜all⌝ the mysteries had not yet been shown to you. You knew worthless things, and in the hardness of your hearts you told them to the women — and through these mysteries women and men do great evil on earth."
4. So say to them: "You have no peace."

Chapter 17

1. They took ⌜and brought⌝ me to a place where those who were there were like flaming fire — when they wished, they looked like men.
2. They brought me to the place of darkness, and to a mountain whose top reached to heaven.
3. I saw the places of the lights of heaven ⌜and the storehouses of the stars⌝, and of the thunder — ⌜and⌝ in the deepest depths, where there were a fiery bow and arrows and their quiver, ⌜and a fiery sword⌝, and all the lightnings.
4. They took me to the living waters, and to the fire of the west, which receives the sun at every setting.
5. I came to a river of fire, where the fire flows like water and pours itself into the great sea in the west.
6. I saw the great rivers and came to the great ⌜river, to the great⌝ darkness, and went where no flesh walks.
7. I saw the mountains of the darkness of winter, and the place where all the waters of the deep flow from.
8. I saw the mouths of all the rivers of the earth, and the mouth of the deep.

Chapter 18

1. I saw the storehouses of all the winds — I saw how He had stocked with them the whole creation and the firm foundations of the earth.
2. I saw the cornerstone of the earth. I saw the four winds that carry [the earth and] the sky of heaven.
3. ⌜I saw how the winds stretch out the arches of heaven⌝ — they stand between heaven and earth: ⌜these are the pillars of heaven⌝.
4. I saw the winds of heaven that turn and bring the sun and all the stars round to their setting.
5. I saw the winds on the earth carrying the clouds. I saw ⌜the paths of the angels — I saw⌝ them at the end of the earth, the sky of heaven above.
6. I went on and saw a place that burns day and night — seven mountains of magnificent stones there, three to the east and three to the south.
7. Of those to the east, ⟨one⟩ was of coloured stone, one of pearl, one of jacinth; those to the south were of red stone.
8. But the middle one reached to heaven like God's throne — of alabaster, its top of sapphire.
9. I saw a flaming fire. And beyond these mountains
10. is a region, the end of the great earth — there the heavens were finished.
11. I saw a deep abyss, with columns ⌜of heavenly fire — and among them I saw columns⌝ of fire falling, beyond measure, up to the height and down to the depth.
12. Beyond that abyss I saw a place with no sky of heaven above it and no solid earth beneath — no water on it, no birds, a waste, a horrible place.
13. I saw seven stars there like great burning mountains. When I asked about them,
14. the angel said: "This place is the end of heaven and earth. It has become a prison for the stars and the army of heaven.
15. The stars that roll over the fire are the ones that broke the Lord's command when they first rose — they did not come out at their appointed times.
16. He was angry with them and tied them up till the time their guilt is complete — (even) ⌜for ten thousand years⌝."

Chapter 19

1. And Uriel said to me: "Here will stand the angels who joined themselves to women. Their spirits take many different shapes — they are defiling mankind and will lead them astray into sacrificing to demons ⌜as gods⌝, (here they will stand), till ⌜the day of⌝ the great judgement, when they will be judged till they are wiped out.
2. And the women of the angels who went astray will become sirens."
3. And I, Enoch, alone saw the vision — the ends of all things. No man will see as I have seen.

Chapter 20

1. These are the names of the holy angels who watch.
2. Uriel — one of the holy angels, over the world and over Tartarus.
3. Raphael — one of the holy angels, over the spirits of men.
4. Raguel — one of the holy angels, who †takes vengeance on† the world of the lights.
5. Michael — one of the holy angels, namely the one set over the best part of mankind ⌜and⌝ over chaos.
6. Saraqâêl — one of the holy angels, set over the spirits who sin in the spirit.
7. Gabriel — one of the holy angels, over Paradise, the serpents, and the Cherubim.
8. Remiel — one of the holy angels, whom God set over those who rise.

Chapter 21

1. I went on to where things were chaotic.
2. I saw something horrible there — no heaven above, no solid earth below, just a chaotic, horrible place.
3. Seven stars of heaven were bound together in it, like great mountains, burning like fire.
4. Then I said: "For what sin are they bound? Why were they thrown in here?"
5. Then Uriel — one of the holy angels, who was with me and was chief over them — said: "Enoch, why do you ask? Why are you so eager for the truth?
6. These are some of the stars ⌜of heaven⌝. They broke the Lord's command and are tied here till their time is complete — the ten thousand years, the time their sins have earned."
7. From there I went to another place, even more horrible than the first. I saw a horrible thing: a great fire burning and blazing there. The place was split open down to the abyss, full of great columns of fire coming down. I could not see how far or how big it was — I could not even guess.
8. Then I said: "How fearful this place is — how terrible to look at!"
9. Then Uriel — one of the holy angels with me — answered and said to me: "Enoch, why are you so afraid and frightened?" And I answered: "Because of this fearful place, and the sight of the pain."
10. And he said ⌜to me⌝: "This place is the prison of the angels. Here they will be locked up for ever."

Chapter 22

1. [And.] From there I went to another place. He showed me in the west ⌜another⌝ great high mountain [and] of hard rock.
2. In it were †four† hollow places — deep, wide, very smooth. †How† smooth the hollow places are — deep and dark to look at.
3. Then Raphael — one of the holy angels with me — answered and said to me: "These hollow places were made for this very purpose: that the spirits of the souls of the dead should gather there — yes, that all the souls of the children of men should gather here.
4. These places were made to receive them till the day of their judgement, till their appointed time [till the period appointed], till the great judgement (comes) on them."
5. I saw the spirits of the children of men who were dead. Their voice went up to heaven, pleading.
6. Then I asked Raphael, the angel with me: "This spirit — whose is it, whose voice goes up pleading?"
7. He answered: "This is the spirit that left Abel, whom his brother Cain killed. He pleads against him till his seed is wiped from the face of the earth, his seed destroyed from among the seed of men."
8. Then I asked about it, and about all the hollow places: "Why is one separated from the other?"
9. He answered me: "These three were made so the spirits of the dead could be separated. Such a division was made ⟨for⟩ the spirits of the righteous — where the bright spring of water is.
10. Such was made for sinners when they die and are buried in the earth, and judgement was not carried out on them in their lifetime.
11. Here their spirits will be set apart in this great pain till the great day of judgement — punishment and torment of those who †curse† for ever, retribution for their spirits. There He will tie them for ever.

Chapter 72

1. The moon rises and sets in these same portals, and so do the leaders of the stars and those they lead — six in the east and six in the west, each following the other in exactly matching order. There are also many windows to the right and left of these portals.

5. The wind drives the chariot on which he climbs. The sun goes down from the sky and comes back through the north to reach the east, and is guided so that he comes to the right (that is, "that") portal and shines in the face of the sky.
6. In this way he rises in the first month in the great portal, which is the fourth [of those six portals in the east].
7. In that fourth portal, from which the sun rises in the first month, are twelve window-openings, and a flame comes out of them when they are opened in their season.
8. When the sun rises in the sky, he comes out through that fourth portal thirty mornings in a row, and sets exactly in the fourth portal in the west of the sky.
9. During this time the day gets longer every day and the night shorter every night, till the thirtieth morning.
10. On that day the day is longer than the night by a ninth part: the day comes to exactly ten parts and the night to eight parts.
11. The sun rises from that fourth portal and sets in the fourth, then goes back to the fifth portal of the east for thirty mornings, rising from it and setting in the fifth portal.
12. Then the day gets longer by †two† parts and comes to eleven parts, and the night gets shorter and comes to seven parts.
13. It goes back to the east and enters the sixth portal, rising and setting in the sixth portal for one-and-thirty mornings, because of its sign.
14. On that day the day gets longer than the night — the day becomes double the night: the day becomes twelve parts and the night is shortened and becomes six parts.
15. Then the sun climbs back to make the day shorter and the night longer: the sun goes back to the east, enters the sixth portal, and rises from it and sets for thirty mornings.
16. When the thirty mornings are done, the day shrinks by exactly one part and becomes eleven parts, and the night seven.
17. The sun goes out from that sixth portal in the west, travels to the east, and rises in the fifth portal for thirty mornings, and sets in the west again in the fifth western portal.
18. On that day the day shrinks by †two† parts and comes to ten parts, and the night to eight parts.
19. The sun goes out from that fifth portal and sets in the fifth portal of the west, then rises in the fourth portal for one-and-thirty mornings, because of its sign, and sets in the west.
20. On that day the day is made equal with the night, [and becomes of equal length]: the night comes to nine parts and the day to nine parts.
21. The sun rises from that portal and sets in the west, goes back to the east, and rises thirty mornings in the third portal, and sets in the west in the third portal.
22. On that day the night gets longer than the day — night gets longer than night and day shorter than day, till the thirtieth morning — and the night comes to exactly ten parts and the day to eight parts.
23. The sun rises from that third portal and sets in the third portal in the west, goes back to the east, and for thirty mornings rises in the second portal in the east, and likewise sets in the second portal in the west of the sky.
24. On that day the night comes to eleven parts and the day to seven parts.
25. On that day the sun rises from that second portal and sets in the west in the second portal, then goes back to the east into the first portal for one-and-thirty mornings, and sets in the first portal in the west of the sky.
26. On that day the night gets longer, twice the day: the night comes to exactly twelve parts and the day to six.
27. (And with that) the sun has crossed the divisions of his orbit and turns back on those divisions of his orbit, entering that portal for thirty mornings and setting in the west across from it.
28. On that night the night has grown shorter by a †ninth† part: the night has become eleven parts and the day seven parts.
29. The sun has gone back and entered the second portal in the east, and travels those divisions of his orbit for thirty mornings, rising and setting.
30. On that day the night grows shorter: the night comes to ten parts and the day to eight.
31. On that day the sun rises from that portal and sets in the west, goes back to the east, and rises in the third portal for one-and-thirty mornings, and sets in the west of the sky.
32. On that day the night shrinks and comes to nine parts, and the day to nine parts; night is equal to day, and the year is exactly three hundred and sixty-four days.
33. The length of the day and of the night, and the shortness of the day and of the night, come about — through the course of the sun these differences are made (that is, "they are separated").
34. So it comes about that his course gets longer by day and shorter by night.
35. This is the law and the course of the sun, and his turning back, as often as he turns back — sixty risings — that is, the great luminary named the sun, forever and ever.
36. What rises (in this way) is the great luminary, named for how it looks, as the Lord commanded.
37. As he rises, so he sets: he does not shrink and does not rest, but runs day and night. His light is seven times brighter than the moon's, but in size the two are equal.

Chapter 73

1. After this law I saw another law, about the smaller light, named the Moon.

Chapter 83

He said to me: "This place you see — here are thrown the spirits of sinners and blasphemers, and of those who do wickedness, and of those who twist everything the Lord has spoken through the prophets — (even) the things that are to come. 7. Some of them are written down in heaven above, so that the angels may read them and know what will happen to the sinners" — and to the spirits of the humble, and of those who punished their bodies and were rewarded by God, and of those who were shamed by wicked men: 8. who love God, and loved neither gold nor silver nor any of the good things in the world, but gave their bodies over to be tortured. 9. From the time they came into being, they did not crave earthly food, but counted everything as a passing breath, and lived that way. The Lord tested them hard, and their spirits were found pure, so that they would bless His name. 10. I have written down in the books all the blessings meant for them. He has given them their reward, because they were found to love heaven more than their life in the world — and though wicked men trampled them underfoot, insulted and mocked them, and shamed them, they blessed Me. 11. Now I will call the spirits of the good who belong to the generation of light, and I will change those who were born in darkness — who in the flesh were not rewarded with the honor their faithfulness deserved. 12. I will bring out into shining light those who have loved My holy name, and I will set each one on the throne of his honor. 13. They will shine for ages without number, for righteousness is God's judgment — to the faithful He will give faithfulness in the home of straight paths. 14. They will see those born in darkness led into darkness, while the righteous shine. 15. The sinners will cry out loud and see them shining, and they will go where days and seasons are appointed for them.
