BASIS: Clean reading edition of Phantasms of the Living by Edmund Gurney, Frederic W. H. Myers, and Frank Podmore (London, 1886, two volumes complete; Internet Archive phantasmsoflivin001gurn and phantasmsoflivin02gurn); spelling and wording original; running headers, signature marks, page numbers and scanner noise removed

Source validation (edition check, done before building):
- Vol. 1 (phantasmsoflivin001gurn) and Vol. 2 (phantasmsoflivin02gurn) title pages read in the djvu text: "ROOMS OF THE SOCIETY FOR PSYCHICAL RESEARCH, 14, Dean's Yard, S.W. TRÜBNER AND CO., LUDGATE HILL, E.C. 1886." Vol. 2 shows Charles E. Lauriat acquisition 11 Sept 1922 (copy history, not a printing date) and a Boston Medical Library stamp.
- The 1918 one-volume issue (PhantasmsOfTheLiving-1918) was inspected and contains only an explicitly ABRIDGED text (its single book file is named "Phantasms-of-the-Living-Abridged.pdf"); it was NOT used. The complete 1886 two-volume text is the text built here, as the title page and this note state.
- The book's own Preface confirms the division of labour: Myers wrote the Introduction and the "Note on a Suggested Mode of Psychical Interaction"; Gurney is solely responsible for the remainder; the collection and appraisal of evidence was joint with Podmore.

Contents: Preface; Chapters I–XII (vol. 1, including Gurney's methodological chapters and the case chapters); Introduction (Myers); Additions and Corrections; Volume II with Further Additions and Corrections, the Supplement, Chapters XIII–XVIII, Myers' "Note on a Suggested Mode of Psychical Interaction" (running-header OCR garbles fixed: CHAPTER XV/CHAPTER XVII numerals), Supplementary chapters and "Cases Received Too Late for Insertion"; the index is not reproduced (noted in text).

Cleaning method: both djvu texts converted with the house converter (font ligatures restored; running heads, signature marks, folios, and page furniture removed; hyphenated line-breaks rejoined). Case-class letter heads and signature slugs used as running heads are dropped. Residual OCR noise survives in some case-table matter and near the appendix ends.

This is the complete text, at the largest scale yet attempted in this series (about 751,000 words by the builder's count of the cleaned draft).
