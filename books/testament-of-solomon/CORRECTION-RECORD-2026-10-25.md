## The four sustained faults and their repairs

**Fault 1 — the basis of the translation stated backwards.** The file claimed, three times, that it was "translated afresh" from McCown's 1922 Greek with Conybeare as a cross-check. The desk check defeated the claim on the page: §130 follows Conybeare clause for clause with only the archaisms ironed out; §113 differs from Conybeare by a single modernised word; §§6–9 and 13–15 follow him sentence by sentence; even the section numbers are Conybeare's own apparatus. The readings genuinely taken from McCown — the two added decans Oropel and Aldameoth, flagged in brackets where they stand, and the name Rhyax where Conybeare reads Ruax — are readings taken *into* a Conybeare base, the opposite of the printed claim. All three printings are corrected:

- Line 7 now reads: "Basis: A modernisation of F. C. Conybeare's English translation, *Jewish Quarterly Review* XI (1898), pp. 1–45, with select readings compared against the Greek text as edited by C. C. McCown, *The Testament of Solomon* (Leipzig: Hinrichs, 1922)."
- Line 9 now reads: "Modernised from Conybeare's translation — Tilford edition."
- The Sources list now names Conybeare first, "The base of this edition, modernised throughout," and McCown second, "Greek critical edition; select readings taken from it, flagged in brackets where they stand in the text." Both Internet Archive identifiers stand unchanged.

**Fault 2 — fifty-nine unlabelled editorial synopsis lines standing inside the text.** Twenty-three "card" lines in the demon catalogue (§10 at old line 44 through §125 at old line 412) and thirty-six "Decan" lines (§§73–106) — one-line summaries in the editor's words, each set immediately before the passage it summarises, reusing that passage's number, with nothing in the book to say they are summaries. Every one of the 59 now stands in square brackets and labelled: "[Synopsis — §10 Ornias — …]", "[Synopsis — Decan 1 — Rhyax (§73) — …]". The summary text itself is kept verbatim inside the brackets — nothing the edition carried is lost, and no summary can now be mistaken for translated text. Square brackets are this book's own existing convention for editorial matter (the bracketed McCown notes at the Oropel and Aldameoth decans), so the mark is the book's own, extended.

**Fault 3 — unlabelled editorial narration at line 218.** Between the §73 paragraph and the first decan, the editor spoke in his own voice — "Each of the thirty-six now speaks in turn. The pattern is the same: …" — with no bracket, heading, or mark to separate him from Solomon. The sentence now stands as "[Editor's note — Each of the thirty-six now speaks in turn. … ]", its wording verbatim, its speaker labelled. The speaker tag (§73's paragraph), the running text, and the editorial commentary now stand cleanly apart.

**Fault 4 — leftover template text at line 3.** The line read "Rare & Obscure Books series — No. ___", the series number never filled in. No series number for this book exists to fill in, so, following the standing precedent of the Acts of John and Apocalypse of Peter repairs of the same day, the placeholder is removed rather than completed with an invented number; the line now reads "Rare & Obscure Books series."

## Judgment calls disclosed

- **The synopsis lines are marked, not removed.** The desk check's candidate repair allowed either. Marking was chosen so the edition loses nothing it carried and the summaries — which do serve a reader as a finding aid — remain available under an honest label. The choice is Robert's to reverse at the eyeball gate; removal would be a further edit, not a correction of this one.
- **One new front-matter paragraph was added** (the 2 inserted lines): "Editorial marks in this edition: one-line summaries written by the editor stand before some sections, set in square brackets and labelled "Synopsis"; they are the editor's summaries, not part of the Testament. Other editorial matter in the body stands in square brackets likewise." A label no reader is told about is only half a label; the note tells him. It stands in the front matter only and asserts nothing about the text.
- **The Sources entries were reordered** (Conybeare before McCown) as part of correcting their roles, so the list's order matches the basis it now states. No fact in either entry changed; the identifiers are untouched.
