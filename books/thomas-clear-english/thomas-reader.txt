The Gospel of Thomas: A Clear English Translation

DRAFT — working translation from the Coptic (and Greek where it survives), prepared with AI assistance; not reviewed by a Coptic scholar; not finished. Corrections invited and credited.

Prologue

These are the hidden sayings that the living Jesus spoke, and Didymus Judas Thomas wrote them down.

Saying 1

And he said: Whoever finds the meaning of these sayings will not taste death.

Saying 2

Jesus said: Let the one who seeks keep on seeking until he finds. When he finds, he will be troubled. When he has been troubled, he will marvel, and he will rule over everything.

Saying 3

Jesus said: If those who lead you say to you, "Look, the kingdom is in heaven," then the birds of the sky will get there before you. If they say to you, "It is in the sea," then the fish will get there before you. But the kingdom is inside you and outside you. When you know yourselves, then you will be known, and you will understand that you are the children of the living Father. But if you do not know yourselves, then you live in poverty, and you yourselves are that poverty.

Saying 4

Jesus said: The old man in his days will not hold back from asking a little child of seven days about the place of life, and he will live. For many who are first will become last, and they will become a single one.

Saying 5

Jesus said: Know what is in front of your face, and what is hidden from you will be shown to you. For there is nothing hidden that will not be made plain.

Saying 6

His disciples asked him and said to him: Do you want us to fast? And how shall we pray and give to the poor? And what rule for food shall we keep? Jesus said: Do not tell lies. And what you hate, do not do it. For everything is laid bare in front of heaven. For there is nothing hidden that will not be shown, and nothing covered that will stay covered without being shown.

Saying 7

Jesus said: Blessed is the lion that the man will eat, and the lion will become a man. And cursed is the man that the lion will eat, and the lion will become a man.

Saying 8

And he said: The man is like a wise fisherman. He threw his net into the sea. He pulled it up from the sea, full of small fish among them. The wise fisherman found a large good fish. He threw all the small fish down into the sea. He chose the large fish without trouble. Whoever has ears to hear, let him hear.

Saying 9

Jesus said: Look, the sower went out. He filled his hand. He threw. Some fell on the road; the birds came and gathered them. Others fell on the rock; they did not take root down into the earth and did not send up ears to heaven. And others fell on the thorns; they choked the seed and the worm ate them. And others fell on the good earth, and it gave good fruit up to heaven; it came sixty per measure and a hundred twenty per measure.

Saying 10

Jesus said: I have thrown fire on the world. And look, I am guarding it until it blazes.

Saying 11

Jesus said: This heaven will pass away, and the one above it will pass away. And those who are dead are not alive, and those who are alive will not die. What you eat when it is dead, you make alive. When you come to be in the light, what will you do? On the day you were one, you made the two. But when you come to be two, what will you do?

Saying 12

The disciples said to Jesus: We know that you will go away from us. Who will become great over us? Jesus said to them: In the place you have come to, you will go to James the Just, the one for whose sake heaven and earth came to be.

Saying 13

Jesus said to his disciples: Compare me, and tell me who I am like. Simon Peter said to him: You are like a just angel. Matthew said to him: You are like a wise philosopher. Thomas said to him: Teacher, my mouth will not take it at all, to say who you are like. Jesus said: I am not your teacher. Since you drank, you got drunk from the spring that bubbles, the one I measured out. And he took him and drew back, and said three sayings to him. When Thomas came to his companions, they asked him: What did Jesus say to you? Thomas said to them: If I tell you one of the sayings he told me, you will pick up stones and throw them at me, and fire will come out of the stones and burn you.

Saying 14

Jesus said to them: If you fast, you will bring sin on yourselves. And if you pray, they will condemn you. And if you give to the poor, you will do harm to your spirits. And if you go into any land and walk in the country, and they take you in, eat what they set before you. Heal the sick among them. For what goes into your mouth will not make you unclean, but what comes out of your mouth — that will make you unclean.

Saying 15

Jesus said: When you see the one who was not born of woman, fall on your faces and worship him. That one is your Father.

Saying 16

Jesus said: Maybe people think I came to throw peace on the world, and they do not know that I came to throw divisions on the earth: fire, sword, war. For there will be five in a house: three against two and two against three, the father against the son and the son against the father. And they will stand, being single ones.

Saying 17

Jesus said: I will give you what no eye has seen, and what no ear has heard, and what no hand has touched, and what has not come up into the heart of a person.

Saying 18

The disciples said to Jesus: Tell us how our end will be. Jesus said: Have you uncovered the beginning, then, that you should look for the end? For where the beginning is, there the end will be. Blessed is the one who will stand in the beginning. He will know the end, and he will not taste death.

Saying 19

Jesus said: Blessed is the one who was, before he came to be. If you become my disciples and hear my words, these stones will serve you. For you have five trees in Paradise that do not stir in summer or in winter, and their leaves do not fall. Whoever knows them will not taste death.

Saying 20

The disciples said to Jesus: Tell us what the kingdom of the heavens is like. He said to them: It is like a grain of mustard seed. It is smaller than all the seeds. But when it falls on ground that is worked, it puts out a big branch, and it becomes a shelter for the birds of the sky.

Saying 21

Mariham said to Jesus: Who are your disciples like? He said: They are like small children staying in a field that does not belong to them. When the owners of the field come, they will say: Let go of our field for us. They take off their clothes in front of them, so they can let it go for them, and they give them their field. Because of this I say: If the master of the house learns that the thief is coming, he will keep watch before he comes, and he will not let him dig into his house — the house of his kingdom — to carry away his goods. But you, keep watch in front of the world. Tie yourselves at your sides with great power, so that thieves will not find a way to come to you, because the very chance you are watching for is the one they will find. Let a person who knows be among you. When the fruit grew ripe, he came in haste with his sickle in his hand, and he cut it. Whoever has ears to hear, let him hear.

Saying 22

Jesus saw little children nursing. He said to his disciples: These little ones who are nursing are like the ones who go into the kingdom. They said to him: Then, if we are little, will we go into the kingdom? Jesus said to them: When you make the two into one, and you make the inside like the outside and the outside like the inside, and the top like the bottom, and when you make the male and the female into a single one, so that the male is not male and the female is not female, when you make eyes where an eye was, and a hand where a hand was, and a foot where a foot was, and a likeness where a likeness was, then you will go into the kingdom.

Saying 23

Jesus said: I will pick you, one out of a thousand and two out of ten thousand, and they will stand, being a single one.

Saying 24

His disciples said: Show us the place where you are, because we must look for you. He said to them: Whoever has ears, let him hear. There is light inside a person of light, and he gives light to the whole world. If he does not give light, it is darkness.

Saying 25

Jesus said: Love your brother like your own soul. Watch over him like the pupil of your eye.

Saying 26

Jesus said: You see the speck in your brother's eye, but the beam in your own eye you do not see. When you throw the beam out of your own eye, then you will see clearly to throw the speck out of your brother's eye.

Saying 27

If you do not fast from the world, you will not find the kingdom. If you do not keep the Sabbath as a Sabbath, you will not see the Father.

Saying 28

Jesus said: I stood in the middle of the world, and I showed myself to them in flesh. I found all of them drunk. I found none of them thirsty. And my soul ached for the children of men, because they are blind in their heart and do not see: empty they came into the world, and empty they seek to leave the world again. But for now they are drunk. When they shake off their wine, then they will change their minds.

Saying 29

Jesus said: If the flesh came to be because of the spirit, it is a wonder. But if the spirit came to be because of the body, it is a wonder of wonders. But I wonder at this: how this great wealth has come to live in this poverty.

Saying 30

Jesus said: Where there are three gods, they are gods. Where there are two or one, I am with him.

Saying 31

Jesus said: No prophet is welcome in his own village. No doctor heals the people who know him.

Saying 32

Jesus said: A city built on a high mountain and made strong cannot fall, and it cannot be hidden.

Saying 33

Jesus said: What you hear in your ear, in the other ear too, shout it out on your rooftops. No one lights a lamp and puts it under a measure, or in a hidden place. He puts it on the lampstand, so that everyone who comes in and everyone who goes out can see its light.

Saying 34

Jesus said: If a blind person leads a blind person, the two of them will fall down into a pit.

Saying 35

Jesus said: No one can go into a strong man's house and take his things by force unless he first ties his hands. Then he can empty his house out.

Saying 36

Jesus said: Do not worry from morning to evening, or from evening to morning, about what you will put on.

Saying 37

His disciples said: On what day will you show yourself to us? And on what day will we see you? Jesus said: When you take off your clothes without being ashamed, and you take your clothes and put them under your feet like little children and trample them, then you will see the son of the Living One, and you will not be afraid.

Saying 38

Jesus said: Many times you have longed to hear these words that I am saying to you, and you have no one else to hear them from. There will be days when you will look for me and will not find me.

Saying 39

Jesus said: The Pharisees and the scribes took the keys of knowledge and hid them. They did not go in, and those who wanted to go in they did not let in. But you, be shrewd like the snakes and innocent like the doves.

Saying 40

Jesus said: A vine was planted outside the Father. And because it is not strong, it will be pulled up by its roots and destroyed.

Saying 41

Jesus said: Whoever has something in his hand, more will be given to him. And whoever does not have, even the little he has will be taken from him.

Saying 42

Jesus said: Be people who pass by.

Saying 43

His disciples said to him: Who are you, that you say these things to us? From the things I say to you, you do not know who I am. But you have become like the Jews: they love the tree, they hate its fruit; they love the fruit, they hate the tree.

Saying 44

Jesus said: Whoever blasphemes against the Father will be forgiven. And whoever blasphemes against the Son will be forgiven. But whoever blasphemes against the Holy Spirit will not be forgiven, either on earth or in heaven.

Saying 45

Jesus said: They do not gather grapes from thorns. And they do not gather figs from brambles, for they bear no fruit. A good man brings out a good thing from his store. A bad man brings out bad things from his bad store that is in his heart, and he speaks bad things. For out of the fullness of the heart he brings out bad things.

Saying 46

Jesus said: From Adam to John the Baptist, among those born of women, there is no one higher than John the Baptist, so that his eyes would not be broken. But I said: whoever among you becomes a little one will know the kingdom, and he will become higher than John.

Saying 47

Jesus said: It is not possible for a man to mount two horses, or to stretch two bows. And it is not possible for a servant to serve two masters — or he will honor the one, and the other he will insult. No man drinks old wine and at once wants to drink new wine. And they do not put new wine into old skins, so that they will not burst. And they do not put old wine into a new skin, so that it will not spoil it. They do not sew an old patch on a new garment, for a tear will come to be.

Saying 48

Jesus said: If two make peace with each other in this one house, they will say to the mountain, "Move away," and it will move.

Saying 49

Jesus said: Blessed are the single ones, and the chosen, for you will find the kingdom — for you are from it, and again you will go there.

Saying 50

Jesus said: If they say to you, "Where did you come from?" say to them, "We came from the light — the place where the light came to be by itself. It stood, and it was revealed in their image." If they say to you, "Is it you?" say, "We are its children, and we are the chosen of the living Father." If they ask you, "What is the sign of your Father in you?" say to them, "It is movement and rest."

Saying 51

His disciples said to him: On what day will the rest for the dead come to be? And on what day will the new world come? He said to them: What you are waiting for has come, but you do not know it.

Saying 52

His disciples said to him: Twenty-four prophets spoke in Israel, and all of them spoke in you. He said to them: You have left the Living One who is in front of you, and you have spoken about the dead.

Saying 53

His disciples said to him: Is circumcision useful, or not? He said to them: If it were useful, their father would father them from their mother already circumcised. But the true circumcision in the spirit has found all the use there is.

Saying 54

Jesus said: Blessed are the poor, for yours is the kingdom of heaven.

Saying 55

Jesus said: Whoever does not hate his father and his mother will not be able to be my disciple. And whoever does not hate his brothers and his sisters and carry his cross as I do will not be worthy of me.

Saying 56

Jesus said: Whoever has known the world has found a corpse. And whoever has found the corpse, the world is not worthy of him.

Saying 57

Jesus said: The kingdom of the Father is like a man who has good seed. His enemy came during the night and sowed weeds on top of the good seed. The man did not let them pull up the weeds. He said to them: In case you go meaning to pull up the weeds and you pull up the wheat along with them — for on the day of harvest the weeds will show plainly; they will be pulled up and burned.

Saying 58

Jesus said: Blessed is the man who has suffered; he has found life.

Saying 59

Jesus said: Look for the Living One while you are alive, so that you will not die and then seek to see him and not be able to see him.

Saying 60

They saw a Samaritan carrying a lamb, going into Judea. He said to his disciples: That one — what is he doing with the lamb? They said to him: So that he may kill it and eat it. He said to them: While it is alive, he will not eat it; but if he kills it, it will become a corpse. They said: He cannot do it any other way. He said to them: You yourselves, seek a place for yourselves in rest, so that you will not become a corpse and be eaten.

Saying 61

Jesus said: Two will rest on a bed. One will die, and one will live. Salome said: Who are you, man, that, as one coming from a single one, you climbed onto my bed and ate from my table? Jesus said to her: I am the one who is from the one who is whole and even. What belongs to my Father has been given to me. I am your disciple. For this reason I say: when he is whole, he will be filled with light; but when he is divided, he will be filled with darkness.

Saying 62

Jesus said: I tell my mysteries to those who are worthy of my mysteries. What your right hand will do, do not let your left hand know what it is doing.

Saying 63

Jesus said: There was a rich man who had much money. He said: I will use my money, so that I may sow and reap and plant and fill my storehouses with fruit, so that I will lack nothing. These were the things he was thinking in his heart. And that night he died. Whoever has ears, let him hear.

Saying 64

Jesus said: A man had guests. And when he had prepared the dinner, he sent his servant so that he might invite the guests. He went to the first. He said to him: My master invites you. He said: I have money with merchants. They are coming to me in the evening. I will go and give them orders. I excuse myself from the dinner. He went to another. He said to him: My master invites you. He said to him: I have bought a house, and they need me for a day. I will have no free time. He came to another. He said to him: My master invites you. He said to him: My friend will have a wedding, and I am the one who will prepare the dinner. I will not be able to come. I excuse myself from the dinner. He went to another. He said to him: My master invites you. He said to him: I have bought a village. I am going to collect the rent. I will not be able to come. I excuse myself. The servant came. He told his master: Those whom you invited to the dinner have excused themselves. The master said to his servant: Go outside to the roads. Whoever you find, bring them, so that they may dine. Buyers and merchants will not go into the places of my Father.

Saying 65

He said: A good man had a place of vines. He gave it to tenant farmers, so that they would work it and he would receive its fruit from them. He sent his servant, so that the tenant farmers would give him the fruit of the place of vines. They seized his servant; they beat him. It was a little more, and they would have killed him. The servant went; he told his master. His master said: Perhaps he did not know them. He sent another servant. The tenant farmers beat that other one too. Then the master sent his son. He said: Perhaps they will be ashamed before my son. Those tenant farmers, since they knew that he is the heir of the place of vines, seized him; they killed him. Whoever has ears, let him hear.

Saying 66

Jesus said: Show me the stone that the builders threw away; it is the corner stone.

Saying 67

Jesus said: Whoever knows everything but falls short in one thing, falls short in everything.

Saying 68

Jesus said: You are blessed when they hate you and persecute you; and they will find no place in the place where they persecuted you.

Saying 69

Jesus said: Blessed are those who were persecuted in their hearts — they are the ones who have come to know the Father in truth. Blessed are the hungry, so that the stomach of the one who desires will be filled.

Saying 70

Jesus said: When you bring forth what is within you, what you have will save you. If you do not have what is within you, what you do not have will kill you.

Saying 71

Jesus said: I will destroy this house, and no one will be able to build it [again — the ending is lost].

Saying 72

A man said to him: Tell my brothers to divide my father's things with me. He said to him: O man, who made me a divider? He turned to his disciples; he said to them: Am I a divider?

Saying 73

Jesus said: The harvest is great, but the workers are few. Pray to the Lord that he send workers out into the harvest.

Saying 74

He said: Lord, there are many around the drinking trough, but there is nothing in the well.

Saying 75

Jesus said: There are many standing at the door, but it is the single ones who will enter the bridal chamber.

Saying 76

Jesus said: The kingdom of the Father is like a merchant who had a load of goods, and he found a pearl. That merchant was wise: he sold the goods and bought the one pearl for himself. You too — seek his treasure, the one that does not fail, that holds: the place where no thief gets near it to eat, and no worm destroys it.

Saying 77

Jesus said: I am the light over all things. I am the all: out of me the all came, and the all reached to me. Split a piece of wood — I am there. Lift up the stone, and you will find me there.

Saying 78

Jesus said: Why did you come out to the field? To see a reed shaken by the wind, and to see a person dressed in soft clothes like your kings and your great men? Those who dress in soft clothes will not know the truth.

Saying 79

A woman in the crowd said to him: Blessed is the womb that bore you, and the breasts that nursed you. He said to her: Blessed are those who heard the word of the Father and kept it in truth. For there will be days when you will say: Blessed is the womb that did not conceive, and the breasts that did not nurse.

Saying 80

Jesus said: Whoever has known the world has found the body. But whoever has found the body — the world is not worthy of him.

Saying 81

Jesus said: Whoever has become rich, let him rule as king. And whoever has power, let him deny it.

Saying 82

Jesus said: Whoever is near me is near the fire. And whoever is far from me is far from the kingdom.

Saying 83

Jesus said: Images are shown to the person. And the light that is in them is hidden in the image of the Father's light. It will be revealed. And his image is hidden by his light.

Saying 84

Jesus said: When you see your likeness, rejoice. But when you see your images which came to be before you — neither dying nor being revealed — you will carry a burden.

Saying 85

Jesus said: Adam came to be from a great power and great wealth, and yet he did not become worthy of you. For had he been worthy, he would not have tasted death.

Saying 86

Jesus said: The foxes have their holes, and the birds have their nests — but the son of man has no place to lay his head and rest.

Saying 87

Jesus said: Wretched is the body that depends on a body, and wretched is the soul that depends on these two.

Saying 88

Jesus said: The angels and the prophets will come to you, and they will give you what is yours. And you yourselves — give them what is in your hands, and say to yourselves: On what day will they come and take what is theirs?

Saying 89

Jesus said: Why do you wash the outside of the cup? Do you not understand that the one who made the inside also made the outside?

Saying 90

Jesus said: Come to me, for my yoke is good and my lordship is gentle — and you will find rest for yourselves.

Saying 91

They said to him: "Tell us who you are, so that we may believe in you."

He said to them: "You examine the face of heaven and earth, and the one standing before you, you do not know — and as for this moment, you do not know how to test it."

Saying 92

Jesus said: "Seek, and you will find. But the things you asked me about in those days, I did not tell you then. Now I want to tell them — and you do not ask about them."

Saying 93

Do not give what is holy to the dogs, lest they throw it to the dung-heap.

Do not throw pearls to the swine, lest they make them …

[The manuscript is damaged here; the sentence breaks off. Nothing is supplied.]

Saying 94

Jesus [said]: "The one who seeks will find. To the one who knocks, it will be opened."

Saying 95

Jesus [said]: "If you have money, do not lend it at interest — but give it to the one from whom you will not take it."

Saying 96

Jesus said: "The kingdom of the Father is like a woman who took a little yeast, hid it in the dough, and made it into big loaves. The one who has ears to hear, let him hear."

Saying 97

Jesus said: "The kingdom of the Father is like a woman carrying a jar full of flour. While she was walking on the road, still far off, the handle of the jar broke, and the flour spilled out behind her on the road. She did not know it; she had felt no weight. When she got inside her house, she set the jar down and found it empty."

Saying 98

Jesus said: "The kingdom of the Father is like a man who wanted to kill one of the great men. He drew the sword in his house and drove it into the wall, to find out whether his hand could drive it all the way through. Then he killed the great man."

Saying 99

The disciples said to him: "Your brothers and your mother are standing outside."

He said to them: "Those here who do the will of my Father — these are my brothers and my mother. They are the ones who will enter the kingdom of my Father."

Saying 100

They showed Jesus a gold coin and said to him: "Caesar's men demand the taxes from us."

He said to them: "Give to Caesar what is Caesar's, give to God what is God's — and what is mine, give to me."

Saying 101

Whoever hates his father and his mother the way I do will not be my disciple.

And whoever loves his father and his mother the way I do will not be my disciple.

For my mother […], but my true mother gave me life.

Saying 102

Jesus said: "Woe to them, the Pharisees — they are like a dog asleep on the oxen's feed: it neither eats nor lets the oxen eat."

Saying 103

Jesus said: "Blessed is the man who knows at what hour, in which part [of the night], the thieves will come in — so that he will get up, gather his kingdom, and gird himself before they come in."

Saying 104

They said to Jesus: "Come, let us pray today and let us fast."

Jesus said: "What then is the sin that I have committed?

Or in what have they defeated me?

But when the bridegroom comes out of the bridal chamber, then let them fast and let them pray."

Saying 105

Jesus said: "Whoever knows the father and the mother will be called the son of a prostitute."

Saying 106

Jesus said: "When you make the two one, you will become sons of man. And when you say, 'Mountain, move away!' — it will move away."

Saying 107

Jesus said: "The kingdom is like a shepherd who had a hundred sheep. One of them went astray — the biggest one. He left the ninety-nine and sought the one, until he found it. When he had toiled, he said to the sheep: 'I want you more than the ninety-nine.'"

Saying 108

Jesus said: "Whoever drinks from my mouth will become like me. I myself will become him, and the hidden things will be revealed to him."

Saying 109

Jesus said: "The kingdom is like a man who had a treasure hidden in his field, and he did not know of it. And after he died, he left it to his son. The son did not know; he took the field and sold it. And the one who bought it came digging, and he found the treasure, and he began to lend at interest to those who wished."

Saying 110

Jesus said: "Whoever has found the world and become rich — let him deny the world."

Saying 111

Jesus said: "The heavens will be rolled up, and the earth will pass away. And the living from the living will not see death." Does not Jesus say: 'Whoever finds himself — the world is not worthy of him'?

Saying 112

Jesus said: "Woe to the flesh that depends on the soul; woe to the soul that depends on the flesh."

Saying 113

His disciples said to him: "On what day is the kingdom coming?"

"It is not coming by watching. They will not say: 'Look, here' or 'Look, there.' But the kingdom of the Father is spread out over the earth — and people do not see it."

Saying 114

Simon Peter said to them: "Let Mary leave us, for women are not worthy of life."

Jesus said: "Look — I myself will guide her, so as to make her male, so that she too may become a living spirit, like you men. For every woman who makes herself male will enter the kingdom of heaven."
