If a marginal note stands on p. 418, it is lost to both witnesses available to the build; the book's count of eleven marginal notes carries that caveat openly, and nothing was supplied for that margin. The text is settled on the edition's parallel-joint evidence and rendered whole; the punctuation discrepancy between the two printings stands unresolved, the print giving both and no witness available to the build deciding between them.

Completeness is carried by the clause-for-clause sample and the marker reconciliation (36 page turns: 31 completion markers, two chunk-boundary carries completed at the next head, three carry-less turns), which excludes duplication mechanically; no missing or repeated text accounts for the extent.
Also disclosed in §7: during chunk 2's work on the 409→410 joint, the EEBO-TCP text of the 1648 volume was fetched once to test whether it could arbitrate the joint.
