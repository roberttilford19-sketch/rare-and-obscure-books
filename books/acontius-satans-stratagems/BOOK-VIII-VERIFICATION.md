# BOOK VIII — VERIFICATION NOTE

Book Eight of the Acontius rebuild (*Stratagematum Satanae*, Oxford 1631; IA item b3033018x) has been translated whole and checked. The book is complete over its true span, and it closes the volume: it begins under its display heading at the head of printed p. 390 and it ends on printed p. 426, where *FINIS.* stands below its last sentence and the rest of the leaf is blank; the leaf that follows opens Acontius's letter to Johann Wolf of Zurich, appendix matter that is no part of the book and is not translated here.
