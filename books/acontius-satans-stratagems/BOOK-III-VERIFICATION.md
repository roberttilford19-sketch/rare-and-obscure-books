# BOOK III — VERIFICATION

**Jacobus Acontius, *Stratagematum Satanae libri octo* (Oxford, 1631) — Book III, fresh English translation from the page images of the 1631 Latin (IA b3033018x), per the frozen Stage 0 control sheet and its dated addendum of 2026-10-25.**

Verification performed 2026-10-25 by the translating crews and the coordinator, against the page images. **Result, with its caveat in the same breath: Book III is complete and stands verified at every check the desk runs — every leaf of the true span present exactly once, every inter-page seam checked, a four-page word-by-word second pass clean on all four pages — and both of the book's boundaries were verified on the images before translation, the start confirming the correction Book II's crew established and the end confirming the control sheet's page but not its position on it. Two inherited statements from Book II's verification do not survive contact with the leaves — the two "excluded" marginal notes it named on pp. 102 and 104 are misdescribed in the one case and nonexistent in the other — and the machine counts in this stretch of the volume are noisy in both directions at leaf level (an italic-heavy leaf under-read by 26 per cent, two show-through leaves over-read by 12–15 per cent). All of this is stated in full below; none of it is hidden in a footnote.** The translation is a DRAFT under the Acontius rebuild's standing fences — no shelf or clone writes, no pushes, no uploads; Book IV has not been started.

## 1. Leaf completeness — mechanically confirmed, on the verified span

**Both boundaries were verified on the page images before translation began**, as the Book III brief required after the control sheet proved fallible on the II/III boundary.

*Start.* Leaf n128 (printed p. 101) was read on the image at the desk. It opens with the completion of Book II's final sentence ("…cum satis multa dicta sint, de authoritate deinceps est dicendum."), and immediately below stands the heading *SATANÆ STRATAGEMATVM / LIBER TERTIVS* with Book III's text opening under a drop-initial C ("CVM authoritatem qua erroribus…"); the page's running head reads TERTIVS and its signature is G 3. This confirms the correction recorded in Book II's verification: **Book III begins mid-p. 101**, not at the foot of p. 104 as the control sheet states. The error and its evidence are recorded in the dated addendum `acontius-stage0/CONTROL-SHEET-ADDENDUM-2026-10-25.md`, which also carries the corrected Book II denominator (7,652 in place of 8,161).

*End.* Leaves n207 and n208 were read on the images at the desk. On n207 (printed p. 180; running head "180 LIBER"), Book III's final sentence completes in two lines at the head of the page ("…bis non animadvertentibus: & qua ratione id ne fiat, cavendum sit, dicemus." — 11 counted words), the heading *SATANÆ STRATAGEMATVM / LIBER QVARTVS* stands immediately below, and Book IV opens there under a drop-initial Q. Leaf n208 (printed p. 181) is Book IV throughout (running head QVARTVS, signature M 3). The control sheet's page for the III/IV boundary (p. 180) is therefore **correct**, but its placement of the Book IV heading at the *foot* of that page is not: the heading stands near the head, and all but two lines of p. 180 are Book IV. **Book III's true span is: printed p. 101 (from the LIBER TERTIVS heading) through the head of printed p. 180** — leaves n128–n207.

**Completeness on the true span.** The translation's structure was checked mechanically: **79 page sections, printed pp. 101–179, each exactly once** (pp. 101–114, 14 sections; 115–127, 13; 128–140, 13; 141–153, 13; 154–166, 13; 167–179, 13), plus the p. 180 completion carried inside p. 179's section by the project's carry convention (a sentence is rendered where it completes), exactly as Book II's p. 101 completion was carried inside its p. 100 section. Every leaf of the span is thereby accounted for: leaves n128–n206 by their page sections, leaf n207 by the completion carried in p. 179's section and by the exclusion record below.

**Exclusions, named.** On p. 101, the two lines at the head closing Book II are excluded (they are translated in Book II). On p. 180, everything from the LIBER QVARTVS heading down is Book IV and none of it was translated into this book. No sentence is translated twice anywhere in the book: at every shared or crossing boundary the completion stands in exactly one book, in exactly one section.

**One inherited error, corrected on the record.** Book II's verification (§1) named two Book III marginal notes standing on the leaves it excluded: on p. 102, "on emissaries sent to learn what is done elsewhere," and on p. 104, "on the godly man's duty toward others erring in religion." Neither description survives the leaves. The note on p. 102 exists but reads *Distinguenda sedulo Capita fidei necessaria & certa à controversis* — on distinguishing the necessary heads of the faith from the controverted ones. **Printed p. 104 carries no marginal note at all**: its margins are blank in this copy, re-checked on the image at the desk during this verification, the whole page read. The notes Book III actually carries in that stretch stand on pp. 101 (two), 102, 106 (two), and 112, and are inventoried in §5. Book II's verification is not rewritten — its build records are appended to, never written over — but its two descriptions are corrected here, where the leaves were read for this book.

## 3. Seam audit — inter-page sentence carries

Every page turn in the book is a logged carry or a logged clean break; the consolidated hard-readings list carries each with its disposition (word splits completed whole and counted once where they begin; catchwords identified as catchwords and never translated twice). The five joins between the six build ranges were cross-checked from **both** sides' logs, and the two sides agree on every join:

- **114/115:** the *insolubili* sentence, split at the catchword *bili*, completes at the head of p. 115 ("…omnes essent Geometrae, omnes Arithmetici, omnes Musici & Astrologi."); it is rendered in p. 114's section, and p. 115's section begins with the next sentence. Both logs quote the same Latin.
- **127/128:** the *Quibus testimoniis docemur…* sentence completes at the head of p. 128; rendered in p. 127's section (with the Act. 4 marginal note in place), excluded at p. 128's head, whose section begins *Sed eum recte Iesum Dei filium credere…* Both logs agree, and the completion's full Latin is quoted in the later chunk's first entry.
- **140/141:** the completion at p. 141's head (*la necessaria causa? Veh illis.*, the word *nulla* split across the turn) is rendered in p. 140's section and excluded at p. 141, whose section begins *Quod si non satisfacit…*
- **153/154:** the completion at p. 154's head (*sis tempus fieri noluerit: tam profectò per homines fieri noluit, quàm per angelos*) is rendered in p. 153's section — whose closing clause, *aut messis tempus*, has no expressed verb in the print and is rendered as printed, the tentativeness stated in the list — and excluded at p. 154, whose section begins *Quòd si magistratus quilibet aggrediatur…*
- **166/167:** p. 167's opening words (*virorum incolumitati?*) are the completion of p. 166's last sentence, rendered there (the p. 166 section was completed from leaf n194) and excluded at p. 167, whose section begins with the next sentence.

Within the ranges, the same discipline held page by page at build time — in this book nearly every page turn carries a sentence (the one clean break inside a range, p. 178 → p. 179, is logged as such). The integrated body was searched mechanically: it contains **no ellipsis characters and no bracketed matter other than the 47 marginal notes** — nothing is silently bridged, summarised, or cut.

## 4. Second-pass sample — four pages, word by word against the images

Four pages spread across the book were re-read independently at verification, word by word, against the page images, with hand counts taken under Token-Cleaning Rule v1.0 from the coordinator's own transcriptions of the leaves:

- **Printed p. 110 (leaf n137): CLEAN.** Hand count **160** against the leaf's cleaned djvu **161** — the two counts reconcile within a word. The translation renders the page throughout, including the tentative *quam … tam* construction logged at build; the completion of p. 109's last sentence at the page's head is correctly absent from this section (it stands in p. 109's), and the closing question is completed from p. 111 as logged. No marginalia stand on the page, and none are carried.
- **Printed p. 121 (leaf n148): CLEAN as translation; its machine count UNDER-READS by a quarter.** Hand count **169** against the leaf's cleaned djvu **125** — a gap of 44 words. The mechanism is visible on the leaf: long stretches of the page are set in italic (the Scripture paraphrase), and the OCR stream garbles italic tokens below the Token-Cleaning Rule's two-letter threshold, losing them to the count. The translation itself reconciles word by word: the innumerable multitude, Paul's knowing-in-part, the disciples' not yet attaining the spiritual kingdom or the Gentiles' inclusion, and the closing note-worthy sentence completed from p. 122 — all present, both marginal notes (*Apoc. 7.*; *1. Cor. 13.*) in place. The hand count governs for this leaf.
- **Printed p. 138 (leaf n165): CLEAN as translation; its machine count OVER-READS on show-through.** Hand count **171** against the leaf's cleaned djvu **197** — a surplus of 26 ghost words. The leaf is heavily show-through-marked (the verso's text is legible behind nearly every line), and ghost tokens of two or more letters survive the cleaning rule, exactly as on Book II's p. 99. The translation reconciles word by word: the page's head is the completion of p. 137's sentence, rendered there; from *Cum credant igitur* down, everything is present in order, including the eucharistic-presence argument and the closing exhortation completed from p. 139. The print error logged at build — *pateant* printed for *pareant* in the parenthesis — was re-read and is **confirmed**: the translation follows the sense ("obey"), and the printed form stands in the list.
- **Printed p. 170 (leaf n197): CLEAN as translation; its machine count OVER-READS on show-through.** Hand count **182** (marginal note included) against the leaf's cleaned djvu **204** — a surplus of 22. The translation reconciles word by word; the marginal note (*Certe telum unum unicumq; confodiendis haeresibus, Dei verbum.*) is carried in place; the two readings logged at build — *terriculamenra* for *terriculamenta* and *quim* — were re-read and are both **confirmed as printed**, translated on the sense with the printed forms named in the list.

Pooled across the sample: hand counts 682 against djvu cleaned 687 — the machine total runs only 0.7 per cent high, but that near-agreement is an artefact of cancellation: the leaf-level errors run from −26 per cent to +15 per cent in both directions, with named mechanisms (italic collapse downward; show-through ghosts upward). No pooled adjustment has been applied to the denominators in §2 on the strength of four leaves; the figures there stand as stated, with this noise sized and on the record — the same caution Book II's verification carried, now documented in both directions.

## 7. Standing and fences

Book III stands as Books I and II stand: a fresh, complete English translation from the 1631 page images, Acontius credited as author, DRAFT banner standing, delivered into the rebuild folder only. No shelf or clone writes were made; nothing was pushed or uploaded; Book IV was not started — though its true opening is now on this record with unusual precision: it begins on printed p. 180, immediately below Book III's two closing lines, under a drop-initial Q ("QVòd cum depravetur doctrina…"), and p. 181 is its throughout. The discrepancies this verification carries openly — the control sheet's Book II/III boundary, wrong by three pages and now corrected by dated addendum; the sheet's placement of the Book IV heading at the foot of a page where it stands at the head; Book II verification's two misdescribed "excluded" notes, one of them on a page whose margins are blank; and machine counts that err by up to a quarter of a leaf in either direction — are the findings the next stage inherits. Under the house maxim they are not embarrassments to be buried: if a figure can be destroyed by the truth, it deserves to be, and the desk's figures stand only where the pages hold them up.
