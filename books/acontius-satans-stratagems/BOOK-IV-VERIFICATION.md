# ACONTIUS REBUILD — VERIFICATION OF THE BOOK IV TRANSLATION

**Book:** Jacobus Acontius, *Stratagematum Satanae libri octo*, Book IV.
**Deliverable verified:** `BOOK-IV-TRANSLATION.md` in this folder — a fresh translation of the whole of Book IV from the 1631 Latin, made leaf by leaf from the page images.
**Source copy:** Internet Archive item `b3033018x` (Oxford, 1631; EEBO copy, British Library, Wing A442) — page images the only copy-text, per the Stage 0 discipline.
**Standing documents applied:** `acontius-stage0/CONTROL-SHEET.md` (frozen), `CONTROL-SHEET-ADDENDUM-2026-10-25.md`, `FORK-RULING-2026-10-25.md`, `TOKEN-CLEANING-RULE.md` v1.0.

**Headline result, with its caveat in the same breath:** Book IV is translated entire — every leaf of its span is present in the scan and accounted for in the translation, both of its boundaries were verified on the page images before and during the build (start mid-p. 180, as the corrected record states; end mid-p. 221, exactly where the control sheet puts it), the 192→195 pagination joint that falls inside this book is verified on the images three separate ways, and a four-page word-by-word second pass found no dropped clause, wrong referent, or bent sense — and this is a verification of completeness and counted extent against the stated denominators, not a certification of the translation's quality, which only an outside Latinist's reading could give. The book's judgment calls and the discrepancies that would not reconcile are stated in §§6–7, not smoothed over.

## §1. The span: both boundaries verified on the images, and the joint

**Start (verified twice, independently).** Book IV begins in the middle of printed p. 180 (leaf n207). The head of that page carries the two closing lines of Book III ("…nobis non animadvertentibus: & qua ratione id ne fiat, cavendum sit, dicemus."), with the running head "QVARTVS. 180" already standing over them; immediately below stands the centred drop heading "LIBER / QVARTVS." and Book IV's text opens with a drop initial, "QVòd cum depravetur doctrina…". The translation's p. 180 section contains Book IV's portion of the page only and opens with the rendered heading line *The Stratagems of Satan. Book Four.* The boundary was verified on the image by the verification desk before the build and again by Chunk 1 against the leaf in the course of translating it; the two reads agree in every particular. Printed p. 181 is Book IV throughout. This is the boundary as corrected in Book III's verification (the frozen sheet put the heading at the foot of p. 180; it stands near the head); the correction already stands in the control-sheet addendum, §3, and Book IV adds nothing to it.

**End (verified twice, independently — and the control sheet is right).** Book IV ends in the middle of printed p. 221 (leaf n246). Its last sentence closes "…deinceps vt morum solvatur disciplina, vtque quo minus id fiat, sit cavendum, sequitur vt dicamus." — the translation's final words are "…it follows that we should next speak of how the discipline of morals is loosened, and how care is to be taken that this should happen the less." Immediately below stands the centred display heading "LIBER / QVINTVS." and Book V's opening ("CONTRA legem qui committunt, difficillimum est, recta illos incedere via.", with its marginal note "An virtutes & vitia in medio sita sint.") — none of it translated here. Leaf n247 (printed p. 222) was fetched and read to confirm the far side: Book V runs on throughout it, so the boundary at mid-p. 221 is the book's true end and not a leaf-turn artefact. **The control sheet's stated end position (mid-p. 221, leaf n246) is correct as printed** — unlike the II/III boundary and the III/IV heading position, it needed no correction, and no further entry in the control-sheet addendum arises from Book IV.

**The pagination joint (p. 192 → p. 195), verified on the images.** This book contains the edition's pagination skip. The facts, read on this copy's images by the translation crew and independently by the verification desk, and identical in both reads: printed p. 192 (leaf n219) closes with a completed sentence — "…nulla præclara habent prudentiæ pietatisq; exercitia." — with a full stop, and the word "Sed" standing at its foot as catchword; printed p. 195 (leaf n220) bears the running head "QVARTVS. 195" and the signature "N" at its foot, opening gathering N, and opens with that same word "Sed" ("Sed tametsi virtute multò impares…"), its own foot carrying signature-and-catchword "N cat." toward p. 196. No sentence, word, or sense crosses the joint; it is a clean sentence break, and the translation passes across it continuously, as the fork ruling directs. The ruling of 2026-10-25 (desk fork ruling; three copies corroborating — this copy, EEBO Copy A, and Copy B read on its page images) is carried in the translation's front matter in the ruled form, and the joint is recorded in full in the hard readings (Chunk 2, entry 2). There are no printed pp. 193–194, and the translation makes no sections for them.

The span's page sections are exactly the forty that exist: printed pp. 180–192 and 195–221 (pp. 193–194 do not exist in the edition). Mechanical check: the translation was built in four page-ranged chunks; on integration the section set was asserted equal to that forty-page list — it matched exactly, no section missing, none duplicated, none out of order. **Book IV in this copy is complete as it stands** (fork (b), ruled 2026-10-25): no leaf is wanting anywhere in the span, and the ~370-word supply once estimated for a hypothetical missing leaf does not arise.

**English extent.** The integrated reading text, carry markers stripped, is **10,368 words**. The chunk sections as filed sum to 10,748 words; the difference is exactly the 38 carry markers at ten words each (380), so the two figures reconcile to the word. ## §3. Seam audit

The book was built in four page ranges; the three internal seams were audited from both sides' build records (each chunk's carry list and hard readings describe the same crossings):

- **pp. 190/191** — the sentence *Idem de rebus aliis omnibus accidisse, quisquis attentius inspexerit, facile inveniat.* crosses the turn, its verb split *in-/spexerit*; it is completed in Chunk 1's p. 190 section ("…whoever looks more carefully will easily find.") and Chunk 2's p. 191 section opens with the next sentence. Both logs name the same carry and the same catchword. Healed.
- **pp. 201/202** — the sentence *Ubi enim simul veluti in conspectu sunt & Dei verbum, & expositio…* crosses the turn, the word *verbum* standing whole at the foot of p. 201 as catchword and again at the head of p. 202; completed in Chunk 2's p. 201 section ("…so that one may reject it unless it seems suitable."), and Chunk 3's p. 202 section opens with the next sentence, excluding the completion word as the convention directs. Healed.
- **pp. 211/212** — the sentence ending *…si id facere non esse sibi persuasum affirmet* crosses the turn; completed in Chunk 3's p. 211 section ("…namely, that he recant when he is not persuaded."), and Chunk 4's p. 212 section opens with the next sentence (*Plerunq; autem ii habentur turbatores…*). Each side's record of the crossing agrees; the completion words stand once, in Chunk 3. Healed.

Within the chunks, every sentence that crosses a page turn is rendered in full where it begins and marked with the house carry marker; the 38 markers in the filed sections correspond one-for-one with the carry entries in the chunks' lists.

## §7. Fences, discrepancies, and what this verification does not claim

- **Interim crew reports are not used as evidence anywhere in this file — a rule this verification itself had to be corrected by.** During the build, the crews' interim progress reports streamed to the desk carried inflated counts (marginalia reported as 16 and 20 against the files' 2 and 0; hard-reading totals of 44, 34, and 45 against 24, 36, and 26 numbered entries as filed; a per-chunk Latin estimate of ≈ 2,220 words for eleven leaves, more than the leaves hold at this book's measured density) and two items that stand in no file (a word supplied at p. 189, and a bracketed supply at p. 218). The crews' **final** completion reports agree with their files on every count. The first draft of §6 above nevertheless carried the two phantom items and several conjectures taken from the interim stream; when the final reports landed, §§6–7 were checked line by line against the chunk files and corrected — the phantom supply, the phantom deletion, and the unattributed conjectures are removed, and what stands in §6 now is what the files contain. The correction is recorded here, in the open, because it is the same failure this section exists to guard against: a verification that repeated the interim numbers would have published two false inventories and credited the translators with calls they never made. Every figure in this file is taken from the files, from mechanical counts over them, or from the desk's own image work.
- The word-by-word second pass covers four of the book's forty pages, plus both boundary pages in full at §1; the remaining pages stand on the build discipline (images as only copy-text, carries and notes logged per page) and the seam audit, not on a second full reading.
- The Latin denominators carry the leaf-level noise sized in §4; the ratios are stated against each denominator in the open, and none is used as a verdict on fidelity.
- Judgment calls stand where §6 places them: the p. 205 *Teneamus* conjecture, Chunk 2's rendering of the unresolved *tanoperis* sequence on the sense the sentence requires, and the tentative constructions quoted in full were the translators' calls, each disclosed with its basis in the hard readings; the desk has verified the disclosures against the images at the sampled places and altered none of them in silence.
- No shelf or clone writes, no pushes, and no uploads were made in this book's work, and Book V was not begun. Book IV, like Books I–III before it, goes forward to desk acceptance on this record; the shelf replacement and folder rename wait for the single landing after all eight books, per the standing instruction.
