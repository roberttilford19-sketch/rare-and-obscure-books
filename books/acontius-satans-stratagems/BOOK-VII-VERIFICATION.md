# BOOK VII — VERIFICATION NOTE

Book Seven of the Acontius rebuild (*Stratagematum Satanae*, Oxford 1631; IA item b3033018x) has been translated whole and checked. The book is complete over its true span: it begins under its display heading at the head of printed p. 331 and it ends at the foot of printed p. 389, whose text it fills; Book Eight's heading stands at the head of p. 390 — the standing ±1-leaf uncertainty that the control sheet and the scoping addendum attached to the VII/VIII boundary is resolved, one page earlier than the sheet's central estimate, and the resolution is recorded in §1 below and in §7 of the control-sheet addendum. Like Book Six, Book Seven shares no leaf with either neighbour: both its boundaries fall on page turns. Both boundaries were verified on the page images of the copy-text before translation. The English is 15,205 words against 10,019 Latin words on the hand-adjusted true-span count (ratio 1.52; the working band is a single-benchmark heuristic, labelled as such in §2, and the ratios are description, not verdict). Several constructions in the book would not resolve and stand flagged, not smoothed, in §6; and one interim figure of the integrator's own making was wrong and is corrected on the record in §7. The translation is a DRAFT — UNREVIEWED, as its banner states.

## 1. The boundaries, as the leaves show them

**Start — as recorded by the Book VI build, and confirmed.** On leaf n356 (printed p. 331; running head *SEPTIMVS. 331*) stands the display heading *SATANÆ STRATA- / GEMATVM / LIBER SEPTIMVS.*, and Book Seven opens beneath a decorated drop-initial S: "*Sed tandem rerum potitus vbi est satanas, atque omnibus quibus potuit munitionibus…*" Nothing of Book Six stands on this leaf: Book Six's text ends at the foot of p. 330, as its own verification records, and the catchword *SATANÆ* at that page's foot anticipates this heading. The translation's first section carries the whole leaf — heading, opening sentences, and the marginal note in the right margin (*Ne eijciantur errores, ne intret veritas, quo pacto conetur satanas.*).

**End — the ±1-leaf uncertainty resolved, against the sheet's central estimate.** Book Seven's text fills printed p. 389 (leaf n414; running head *SEPTIMVS. 389*, signature *Bb 2*) to the foot of the text block and ends with the sentence "*Scrutamini quæso diligenter, quæ fuerint Pontificii regni quasi incunabula, à quibus initiis, qui ritus, quæ leges, quæ instituta, quæ tyrannides profectæ fuerint, quàm bellè fucatos quæq; res prætextus habuerit, & quo tandem evaserit: nolite committere vt, cum tot, ac tam magna sint ante oculos exempla, non saltem aliorum malo moniti profecisse vos nonnihil, ac prudentiores facti esse videamini.*", closing with a full point, with the catchword *SATANÆ* at the right-hand foot of the page. Book Eight's display heading (*SATANÆ STRATAGEM ATVM / LIBER OCTAVVS.*) stands at the **head of printed p. 390** (leaf n415; running head *390 LIBER*), and Book Eight opens there under a drop-initial Q: "*QVænam esset ad eum quem consiliorum suorum omnium finem sibi Satanas proposuisset, perveniendi via…*". The leaf after (n416, printed p. 391) already carries the running head *OCTAVVS. 391* and Book Eight text throughout, which corroborates the heading's place independently. The frozen sheet's Book VII row ("330–391", closing "~p. 391 (n416), boundary ±1 leaf") and its Book VIII row ("heading ~p. 391") therefore err in their central placement: the uncertainty the sheet honestly carried resolves at its near edge — Book Seven's last page is p. 389, and the heading stands on p. 390. Book Seven is the third book of the rebuild whose far boundary the sheet misplaced (after Books III and V), and, like Book VI before it, it shares no leaf with either neighbour. The correction is appended to the control-sheet addendum as its §7, dated 2026-10-25; the frozen sheet itself is untouched, and the ±1-leaf caveat is discharged — it travels no further, to Book VIII's length or anywhere else. Nothing of Book Eight stands in this translation: the final translated sentence is the *Scrutamini* sentence, rendered "Search, I ask, diligently what were, as it were, the cradles of the Pontifical kingdom, from what beginnings, what rites, what laws, what institutions, what tyrannies proceeded, how beautifully painted pretexts each thing had, and to what it at last came out: do not allow it to happen that, when so many and so great examples are before your eyes, you do not seem to have profited at least something, warned by others' evil, and to have been made more prudent."

## 7. Fences, discrepancies, and what this note does not claim

The fences stood: copy-text was the page images of IA item b3033018x and nothing else; the djvu text served as finding aid and denominator source only; neither the 1648 English nor Curtis (1940) was opened as a base; no shelf or clone file was touched, nothing was committed, pushed, or uploaded; Book VIII does not start — its heading leaf (n415) and the two leaves after it (n416, n417) were read only to fix and corroborate the boundary. The writes outside the build's working files are the page images downloaded into the rebuild directory (`work/book7-images/`, leaves n356–n420), the djvu lines and per-leaf count files for the span (`work/book7-djvu-lines.txt`, `work/book7-per-leaf-djvu.json`), and the boundary-and-denominator correction appended to the control-sheet addendum (its §7), as the standing instruction directs; the frozen control sheet was not rewritten.

Discrepancies, none of them smoothed:

1. **The control sheet's VII/VIII placement is wrong in its central estimate** (§1): Book Eight's heading stands at the head of p. 390, not at about p. 391, and Book Seven's last page is p. 389. The sheet's "∼" and the scoping addendum's ±1-leaf caveat covered the uncertainty; it is now resolved and corrected on the record in addendum §7, and the caveat is discharged.
2. **Stage 0's Book VII denominator spans the wrong leaves at both ends** (§2): the 10,347 figure aggregates leaves n355–n415, taking in Book Six's last leaf and Book Eight's heading page. Unlike Books V and VI, whose Stage 0 figures each also *omitted* a leaf of their own book, this figure omits nothing of Book Seven — its whole error is the two foreign leaves (171 + 125 = 296 words), and the recomputed figures stand in §2.
3. **The djvu stream fails demonstrably on three of the five sampled leaves** (§4), in both directions — inflation by facing-leaf harvest and speck (n373, n399) and loss by fusion and collapse (n414). Hand counts govern on those leaves and are substituted in denominator (b); no global correction factor is applied, the failures being local, opposed in direction, and of unlike size.
4. **The scripture references are rendered in two forms across the chunks** (§5): disclosed, unharmonised, and left to the review pass.
5. **The integrator's first boundary read of leaf n414 was wrong, and its hand count is void.** Before the crews were dispatched, the integrator read leaf n414 from a singly downloaded image and transcribed the page as a single closing period beginning "*Nolite ergo quæso in hoc vsque adeò indulgere vobis…*" — a text this leaf does not carry. The error surfaced at integration, when the delivered chunk 5 file disagreed with that transcription and the leaf was re-read: the file the crews worked from is byte-identical with a fresh download from the Archive, and on it the leaf carries exactly what chunk 5 records, in every particular — the completion word *jiciunt* at its head, the *Vidimus etiam alicubi…* sentence, the *Scrutamini…* close, the signature *Bb 2*, the catchword *SATANÆ*. A hand count of 193 computed on the false transcription was discarded, and the sample count for n414 in §4 (162) was retaken from the second transcription. What the false read touched was confined: the boundary particulars announced at dispatch — that Book Seven ends at the foot of p. 389 with the *…facti esse videamini* clause, the signature, and the catchword — were facts both reads share and the second read verifies, so the boundary resolution, the denominations, and the chunk briefs stood unaffected; the false text entered no delivered file, and its only casualty was the void interim count. It is recorded here in full because the build's discipline — every figure re-derived from the delivered files — is what caught it.
6. **Interim figures, crews, reconciled clean** (the lesson of Book IV's §7, Book V's §7 and Book VI's §7, applied): all fifteen crew-reported figures — five section-word counts, five hard-reading counts, five marginal-note counts — matched the integrator's independent counts from the delivered files exactly, and the marker arithmetic (55 × 10 = 550; 15,755 − 550 = 15,205) closes to the word. The one interim misreport of this build is the integrator's own, at item 5 above.

What this note does not claim: no independent collation of this copy against a second copy of the 1631 edition was performed; the constructions flagged in §6 stand on their stated bases and on nothing stronger. The translation remains a draft, pending the desk's review pass, like Books I–VI at this stage of their builds.
