# BOOK VI — VERIFICATION NOTE

Book Six of the Acontius rebuild (*Stratagematum Satanae*, Oxford 1631; IA item b3033018x) has been translated whole and checked. The book is complete over its true span: it begins under its display heading at the head of printed p. 291 and it ends at the foot of printed p. 330, whose text it fills; Book Seven's heading stands at the head of p. 331, not on p. 330 as the control sheet's table implies — that correction is recorded in §1 below and in §6 of the control-sheet addendum. Book Six is the first book of the rebuild whose two boundaries both fall on page turns: no leaf is shared with either neighbour. Both boundaries were verified on the page images of the copy-text before translation. The English is 10,382 words against 6,716 Latin words on the hand-adjusted true-span count (ratio 1.55; the working band from Books I–III is a single-benchmark heuristic, labelled as such in §2, and the ratios are description, not verdict). One construction in the book would not resolve and stands flagged, not smoothed, in §6. The translation is a DRAFT — UNREVIEWED, as its banner states.

## 1. The boundaries, as the leaves show them

**Start — as recorded, and confirmed.** On leaf n316 (printed p. 291; running head *SEXTVS. 291*, signature T) stands the display heading *SATANÆ STRATAGEM ATVM / LIBER SEXTVS.*, and Book Six opens beneath a decorated initial I: "*Illud constat, Christi regni instaurationem in doctrinæ, hoc est, in Evangelicæ veritatis restitutione esse positam…*" Nothing of Book Five stands on this leaf: Book Five's text ends at the foot of p. 290, as its own verification records, and the catchword *SATANÆ* at that page's foot anticipates this heading. The translation's first section carries the whole leaf — heading, opening sentences, and the marginal note in the right margin (*Opinionum de religione modesta libertas, regno Satanae imprimis adversatur.*).

**End — the sheet's page is right and its heading position is wrong, and the correction is on the record.** Book Six's text fills printed p. 330 (leaf n355; running head *330 LIBER*) to the foot of the text block and ends "…*Neq; cavere, quominus hæc fiant, quisquam possit, nisi in tempore vigilent pastores, & viri pii, prudentesque alii, ne impetratam veritatis lucem sibi eripi denuò patiantur.*", with the catchword *SATANÆ* at the right-hand foot of the page. Book Seven's display heading (*SATANÆ STRATAGEM ATVM / LIBER SEPTIMVS.*) stands at the **head of printed p. 331** (leaf n356; running head *SEPTIMVS. 331*), and Book Seven opens there under a drop-initial S: "*Sed tandem rerum potitus ubi est satanas, atque omnibus quibus potuit munitionibus…*". The frozen sheet's Book VI row ("closes ~foot of p. 330") is therefore correct for this book's close; its Book VII row ("heading ~p. 330"), read together with the scoping addendum's ±1-leaf caveat on the VII/VIII boundaries, resolves at this end to the head of p. 331 — the same one-page-late heading position the rebuild has now met at the III/IV, V/VI, and VI/VII boundaries. The correction is appended to the control-sheet addendum as its §6, dated 2026-10-25; the frozen sheet itself is untouched. Nothing of Book Seven stands in this translation: the final translated sentence is the *Neq; cavere* sentence, rendered "Nor can anyone take heed that these things do not happen, unless pastors and godly men, and other prudent men, watch in good time, lest they suffer the light of truth that has been obtained to be snatched from them again." The ±1-leaf uncertainty that the scoping addendum attaches to Book VII's length now attaches only to that book's far boundary (VII/VIII); its near boundary is fixed by this build.

The rendering is the desk's best construction, openly labelled tentative in its record; a reviewer who reads the construction otherwise has the Latin in front of him in the entry.
