## What this edition is

A clean reading edition of Richet's English treatise on metapsychics — Richet's own survey and argument, in the 1923 De Brath wording, from the definition and history of metapsychics through subjective cryptesthesia (experimental, sporadic monitions, premonitions) and objective phenomena (telekinesis, ectoplasms, levitations, bilocations, hauntings) to the fifteen-part Conclusion. Nothing has been modernized, summarized or rewritten; this is a reprint, not the editor's research and not an endorsement of Richet's conclusions or his critics.

## How the text was cleaned

- Bounds: the edition runs from the title-page block through the end of Book IV, Conclusion XV.
