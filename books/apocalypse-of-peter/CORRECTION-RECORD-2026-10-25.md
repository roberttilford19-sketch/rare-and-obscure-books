## The four sustained faults and their repairs

**Fault 1 — the meaning-reversing change at line 116 (the fig-tree parable).** The shelf read "they will turn away **from** them," reversing James. James, at printed p. 511, reads "they shall turn away **after** them and deny him [whom our fathers did praise], even the first Christ whom they crucified" — to turn away *after* the false Christs is to follow them; *from* them is the opposite, and it silently contradicted James's own note, which the shelf itself retained ("The sense needed is that Israel sees the wickedness of the Antichrist and does not follow him"). Line 116 now reads "they will turn away **after** them," and James's bracket around the supplied clause is restored with it: "deny him **[whom our fathers praised]**, the first Christ, whom they crucified." The retained note now agrees with the sentence it stands beside.

**Fault 2 — the corrupt word filled with an invented reading at line 118.** James, at printed p. 512, prints the word between daggers as corrupt: "and how they †do† that are upright in heart." The shelf had replaced the broken word with the smooth invented phrase "how those who are upright in heart **will be kept**," with no mark or note. That phrase is removed. Line 118 now carries James's clause as he printed it: "how they **†do†** that are upright in heart." Recorded plainly for the eyeball gate: the sentence is broken at that point because James's text is broken there and says so with its obeli; the brokenness is the honest state, restored deliberately, and nothing is invented to smooth it.

**Fault 3 — James's corruption marks and supplied-word brackets silently dropped.** Each instance the desk verified is restored, in this edition's modern wording inside James's marks:
- Line 82 (verse 33, the Akhmim fragment): James brackets the clause as editorial, "[And beside them other men and women]." Line 82 now reads "**[Beside them were other men and women]** holding rods of fire…" James's footnote marker after the bracket is not carried; this edition does not reproduce James's footnote apparatus, retaining only the notes that stand in its text (as at lines 116, 132 and 170). Flagged here for the eyeball gate.
- Line 126: James brackets the closing words as intrusive, "flesh and skin and hair thereon [and soul and spirit]." Line 126 now reads "…flesh, and skin and hair on them **[and soul and spirit]**."
- Line 166 (Gr. 30): James encloses the clause between daggers, "†upon the stone shall be a pillar of fire,†." Line 166 now reads "**†on the stone there will be a pillar of fire,†** and the pillar is sharper than swords."
- Line 188: James sets daggers around the Ezrael clause, "†shall bring them forth out of this fire." Line 188 now reads "Ezrael, the angel of God, **†will bring them out of this fire†** and set a judgment of decision."
- Line 190: James sets daggers around the whole wheels span, "And they that are in the pit shall burn: now these are the sorcerers and sorceresses. Those wheels shall be in all decision (judgement, punishment) by fire without number." Line 190 now carries that span between daggers in this edition's wording: "**†And those who are in the pit will be burned. Now these are the sorcerers and sorceresses. Those wheels will be in every decision — judgment, punishment — by fire without number.†**"
