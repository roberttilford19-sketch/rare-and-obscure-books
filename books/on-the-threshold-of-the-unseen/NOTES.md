## What this edition is

A clean reading edition of Barrett's 1917 examination of the phenomena of spiritualism and the evidence for survival after death — his recast and enlarged successor to *On the Threshold of a New World of Thought* (issued 1908), with the Society for Psychical Research survival evidence brought in. Nothing has been modernized, summarized or rewritten; Barrett's (and Hyslop's, and Balfour Stewart's in Appendix B) wording stands, presented as made, for the reader to judge. It is a reprint, not the editor's research.

## How the text was cleaned

- Bounds: the edition runs from the scanned title page (djvu line 36) through the end of Appendix D (line 12260, "as Plotinus tells us."). Excluded: djvu lines 1–35 (Harvard Medical Library "8 The Fenway" leaf, "Ex Libris William Sturgis Bigelow" bookplate, barcode "3 2044 020 917 555", the Internet Archive digitization leaf "Digitized by tine [for the] Internet Arciiive [for Archive] in 2010 … http://www.archive.org/details/onthresholdofunsOObarr" with letter-O for zeros, and a duplicate of the title) and lines 12261 to end, the Index (pp. 327–336 in the original pagination). The Index is omitted because it is a page-number finder keyed to the 1917 pagination, which this repaginated edition does not preserve; it remains available in the source scan (1,453 words by Python split). The Contents in this edition retains the original page numbers for reference and carries an "Index [not reproduced in this reading edition; see NOTES.md] — 327" line (327 from the first Index folio) so the book's structure is honestly shown. Printer's marks on the title verso ("o", ": /d6.") are dropped; the impression lines themselves are kept in the title block as scanned.
- Running heads, folios and page numbers stripped throughout: 313 folio/head lines dropped in the main pass (e.g. "vii", "viii Preface", "Preface ix", "2 Chapter I", "Introduction 3", "320 Appendix D", "Suggestions for Experimenters 321", "Superstition 303"), with five further garbled folio paragraphs and two folio prefixes removed on audit ("Introduction II" for 11, "IS" for 15, "Scientific Objections 2J" for 29, "The Super-sensible World ill" for 111, standalone "284.", and the prefixes "180 Chapter XIV" [scan "i8o Vhapter XIV"] and "318 Appendix C" [scan "3i8 Appendix C"] stripped from the continuations they headed). Odd-page heads that merely name the page's topic ("Are Apparitions Objective? 147", "Statistical Enquiry 143", "The Stigmata 155") were treated as running heads and dropped, not promoted to headings. Sentences split across page turns were rejoined (where a footnote intervenes at the page foot, the sentence's two halves necessarily stand as separate paragraphs around the note; no words were added or moved).
- Chapter headings converted to Markdown `## ` in the form "Chapter I.
