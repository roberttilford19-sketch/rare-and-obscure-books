## What this edition is

The complete 1903 text of Myers's posthumous summa — the full statement of the "subliminal self" argument and the era's great dossier for survival — with Preface, Editorial Note, Glossary, the Explanation of the Plan of Arrangement, the Syllabuses to every chapter, Chapters I–X with their numbered sections, the appendix case reports ordered to the chapters they illustrate, and the Epilogue's Appendices A–C. The abridgement that most modern readers meet cuts the appendix evidence; this edition keeps it, since the evidence is the book. Nothing has been modernized, summarized or rewritten; Myers's wording stands, presented as made, for the reader to judge. It is a reprint, not the editor's research. The series claim is a clean, complete, honestly labelled reading edition; no scarcity claim is made (the work is much reprinted in the print-on-demand trade).

## How the text was cleaned

- Bounds: Volume One is taken whole from the scan, from the Preface through the Appendices to Chapter VI; Volume Two from its Syllabuses through the Appendices to Chapter IX. Volume Two's Index (djvu lines 37294 onward) is omitted: it is a page-number finder keyed to the 1903 pagination, which this repaginated edition does not preserve; it remains available in the source scan. The Contents printed in this edition retains the original 1903 page numbers for reference and records the omission. The volumes' own divider text "END OF VOL. I" is dropped, and the digitization leaves, bookplates and copyright-deposit stamps of the two scans are dropped.
- Running heads, folios and page numbers stripped throughout (e.g., "102 CHAPTER III [326."). Sentences split across page turns were rejoined; line-break hyphenation closed up (syllable breaks rejoined; true compounds keep their hyphen); the djvu double-spacing between words was collapsed.
- Structure: the two volumes are joined in one reading text with "Volume One"/"Volume Two" dividers (editorial, following the printed two-volume issue). Chapter heads are set as "Chapter I. Introduction" etc. from the printed Contents; the Syllabuses' chapter heads stand as subheads under "Syllabuses", with each chapter's own syllabus order preserved. Chapter III's head stands twice in Volume One, as the scan prints a repeated chapter head partway through that long chapter; both stand as printed. In Volume Two, the syllabus head for Chapter VIII is garbled beyond recovery in the scan, so that chapter's syllabus paragraphs follow Chapter VII's syllabuses without their own subhead (contents verify the material is present in order).
- Headmatter: the printed Contents pages of both volumes are reset here as clean lists with the 1903 page numbers retained; the Glossary and the Explanation of Plan of Arrangement (which explains Myers's section/appendix numbering, §100–§966 A) are kept entire.
- Standalone appendix half-title lines ("APPENDICES / TO / CHAPTER …") are absorbed into the section heads of this edition; footnote calls (scan carets) are stripped as scanner noise while the footnote texts themselves stand as paragraphs.

## Doubtful places — kept honest, nothing invented

- OCR residue: Myers's text is dense with Greek, French and scientific terms; single misread letters in names and foreign titles from the scans may survive here even after the folio and noise passes. Anyone citing a single word or a proper name should check the scan image.
- The Syllabuses are Myers's own numbered abstracts and repeat chapter material in compressed form; their presence alongside the chapters is the 1903 book's own design, not duplication.
- Plates/figures: any illustration references stand as printed; no plates are reproduced in this text edition.
